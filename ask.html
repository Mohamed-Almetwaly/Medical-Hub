What are the causes of persistent eye redness?

Redness can be due to inflammation, allergy, dryness, or strain. It is recommended to see an eye specialist if it lasts more than 48 hours.

Eye — Dr. Sara Ahmed · Ophthalmologist

How do I deal with knee pain while walking?

Rest, reducing weight on the joint, strengthening exercises for the muscles around the knee, and consulting an orthopedic specialist to evaluate the condition and provide appropriate exercises.

Bone — Dr. Karim Hussein · Orthopedic Surgery

What causes tooth sensitivity?

Enamel erosion, exposed tooth roots, or a small cavity can cause sensitivity. Use a specialized toothpaste and schedule a check-up with the dentist.

Teeth — Dr. Manal Samir · Dentist

What are the symptoms of high blood pressure?

Symptoms often do not appear clearly, but sometimes the patient may experience headaches, dizziness, or light nosebleeds. Therefore, it is recommended to monitor blood pressure regularly.

Heart — Dr. Ahmed Ali · Cardiologist

How can I treat dry skin in winter?

Use a moisturizer immediately after showering, avoid hot water, drink plenty of fluids, and consult a dermatologist if dryness is severe or accompanied by itching.

Skin — Dr. Nour Hassan · Dermatologist

When should I go to the emergency room?

Go immediately if you experience chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, or stroke symptoms such as facial drooping or arm weakness.

General — Dr. Mohamed Fathi · Emergency Medicine
